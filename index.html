GKN AEROSPACE · ENTERPRISE AI · 2023–2026

Secure Enterprise GenAI Under ITAR

Making GenAI adoption possible in a regulated aerospace enterprise — without surrendering data sovereignty.

Challenge

GKN wanted the productivity of enterprise GenAI, but adoption had stalled: leadership was deadlocked on a deployment pathway that could satisfy aerospace-grade security, and every default answer assumed the public cloud.

Constraints

ITAR and export-control rules, data-sovereignty requirements, enterprise security review, and stakeholders spread across the UK, Sweden, the Netherlands and India.

What I did

Explored viable adoption pathways and led rapid validation of secure on-premise LLM and RAG architectures — translating technical feasibility, regulatory constraints and enterprise requirements into a scalable adoption pathway, and working with the CDO and global technology stakeholders to build executive support.

Outcome

The board-level deadlock was resolved and cloud dependency reduced by 30%. The work fed investment cases supporting $10M+ in global innovation funding, inside a portfolio delivering $30M+ in quantified business value.

30% less cloud dependency
$10M+ innovation funding supported
4 countries aligned
Shows Regulated innovation Architecture judgement Executive alignment
GKN AEROSPACE · COMPUTER VISION

Computer Vision Inspection: POC in a Month

Turning a manual inspection bottleneck into an industrialisation roadmap — starting with a one-month prototype.

Challenge

Wire-insertion inspection was a fully manual step — slow, repetitive, and consuming skilled hours the business needed elsewhere.

Approach

Identified the step as an automation opportunity, evaluated computer vision and OCR pathways against the real inspection workflow, and chose a YOLO-based detection approach that could be demonstrated fast.

What I did

Built a working YOLO prototype within one month to demonstrate feasibility, then converted the successful validation into an industrialisation roadmap and a scalable solution concept.

Outcome

Projected savings of 500+ manual inspection hours annually, with a clear pathway from proof of concept to production.

1 month idea to working prototype
500+ hours/year projected savings
Shows Rapid experimentation Opportunity discovery POC-to-industrialisation
ABANS FINSERV · AGENTIC AI · 2026

AI-Powered Regression Testing for a Trading Platform

An internal product built with Playwright and Claude Code that turned a week of manual QA into a 2–3 hour automated run.

Challenge

Dealer Pad — a capital-markets trading platform serving 40+ users across deal entry, hedging, ETFs and reconciliation — needed a full regression pass every release. Done manually, that cost up to a week per cycle on a 15-day sprint cadence.

What I did

Identified regression testing as an AI-automation opportunity, then independently conceived and built an internal testing product on Playwright and Claude Code, spanning 80+ test cases with pass/fail and defect reporting.

How AI is embedded

Beyond testing, GenAI now runs through discovery, requirements, ticketing and documentation workflows — compressing discovery-to-delivery turnaround for a six-engineer team.

Outcome

Full regression dropped from up to a week of manual effort to a repeatable 2–3 hour automated run, releasing engineering time back into the sprint.

2–3 hrs full regression (was ~1 week)
80+ automated test cases
40+ platform users
Shows Agentic AI in production 0-to-1 product Hands-on build
